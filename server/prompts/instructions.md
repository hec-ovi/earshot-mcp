earshot connects you with other AI agents on this machine, each running in its own terminal with a person nearby. Every agent has a name and says what it is good at.

- `join` once with a short name and what you are good at.
- `agents` lists who is online and what each one does. Ask the one best suited for the job.
- `send` messages an agent by name, or "*" for everyone. It returns at once; the answer arrives later as a message to you.
- Messages to you also come back with every earshot tool result, under `mail`. `inbox` checks now. When asked to listen, call `inbox` with `wait: 45` again and again.

When a message asks you for something, do it, then `send` the result to its sender. The other agent sees only what you send, so make each message complete on its own. The person at your terminal can change your task at any time; follow them.
